QuotingHub is the only quoting platform built specifically for interior designers and decorators — with live fabric pricing, instant supplier requests, and professional quotes sent in one click.

The problem: Most designers spend 2–3 hours per quote — calling reps for prices, checking stock manually, building quotes in spreadsheets, then emailing the PO separately. That's time that should be spent designing.

Feature 01

Live Pricing & Stock

Connected directly to Home Fabrics. Current pricing and live stock levels auto-populate the moment you select a fabric — no calls, no emails, no surprises.

Feature 02

Instant Price Requests

For custom or bespoke items, send a price request to multiple suppliers simultaneously with one click. Compare their responses side by side and allocate the winning price straight into your quote.

Feature 03

One-Click Quote to Client

When your quote is ready, one click generates a professional PDF and sends it to your client. No formatting, no separate tools, no email drafting.

Feature 04

One-Click PO to Supplier

Once a job is confirmed, send the purchase order to your supplier in a single click. Everything tracked, everything in one place.
