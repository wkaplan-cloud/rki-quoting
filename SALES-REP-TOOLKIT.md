# QuotingHub — Sales Rep Toolkit

---

## What QuotingHub Is

A professional quoting and project management tool built specifically for interior designers and decorators. It covers the full quoting workflow from first price check to client quote to supplier PO — all in one place.

---

## The Problem It Solves

Most designers are running their business across WhatsApp, spreadsheets, and email. They're spending hours per job on admin: checking stock, chasing supplier prices, building quotes manually, and sending POs one by one. QuotingHub eliminates all of that.

---

## The Four Core Features (Know These Cold)

### 1. Live Fabric Pricing + Stock — Home Fabrics
QuotingHub connects directly to Home Fabrics. When a designer selects a fabric, current pricing and live stock levels auto-populate. No calling reps. No quoting fabric that's out of stock. Accurate, instant, every time.

### 2. Price Request — Custom & Bespoke Items
For items that aren't in a catalog, the designer adds a photo and details, selects which suppliers to send to, and clicks once. All selected suppliers receive the pricing request simultaneously. When they respond with a price and timeline, the designer sees everything in one place with a side-by-side comparison. They select the winning supplier and that price flows directly into the quote. No back and forth. No copy-pasting.

### 3. One-Click Quote to Client
When the quote is ready, one click generates a professional PDF and sends it to the client. No separate tools, no formatting, no email drafting.

### 4. One-Click PO to Supplier
Once a job is confirmed, one click sends the purchase order to the supplier. Done.

## Tone Guide

**Peer-to-peer. Not salesy.**
You're not selling enterprise software. You're introducing a designer to a tool that makes their business run properly. Speak like someone who understands their world.

- Say: *"You'll never spend half a day chasing prices before you can quote"*
- Not: *"Our platform streamlines your procurement workflow"*

**What resonates with designers:**
- Time saved per job
- Looking more professional to clients
- No more stock surprises mid-project
- Getting supplier prices fast without the back and forth
- Everything tracked in one place

**What to avoid saying:**
- "All-in-one solution"
- "Streamline your workflow"
- "Enterprise-grade"
- Anything that sounds like accounting software
