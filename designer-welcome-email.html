Welcome — let's build your first quote.

You're all set up. QuotingHub is built around one idea: your time should go to designing, not chasing prices or wrestling with spreadsheets. Here's how to get your first quote out the door in minutes.

Your dashboard — the live view of every project at every stage.
Getting Started — 5 Steps
1
Set up your studio
Go to Settings → Studio Settings (some accounts show this under Admin → Studio Settings) and add your business name, logo, VAT number, and banking details. This info auto-fills every quote and invoice — set it once and forget it.
Studio Settings — business info, VAT, banking details, and quote defaults all in one place.
2
Add your suppliers
Head to Suppliers and add the companies you order from. Set a default markup % per supplier — QuotingHub applies it automatically every time you add a line item from that supplier.
Suppliers — each with a default markup that flows through automatically to your quotes.
3
Add your client
Go to Clients and create a contact. You'll link this client when you start a new project.
4
Create a project & build your quote
Click New Project on the dashboard. Select your client, name the project, and start adding line items — furniture, fabrics, labour, anything you're sourcing. Markup is calculated automatically.

If you have access to the live price list (e.g. Home Fabrics), search and select items directly and prices populate for you.
A project with line items — grouped by room, with quantity, supplier, and markup all tracked.
5
Generate & send your quote
Once your line items are in, hit Email → Quote. A branded PDF is built instantly — totals, VAT, and deposit amount all calculated. Email it to your client directly from QuotingHub and track when it was sent and approved.
A few things worth knowing
  • When your client approves, mark the deposit received — QuotingHub tracks every stage from quote to final payment.
  • Purchase orders are generated automatically, grouped by supplier, and can be emailed directly from the project.
  • Your dashboard shows the full pipeline at a glance: awaiting deposit, in production, and ready to invoice.
  • Default deposit % and VAT rate are set in Studio Settings and can be overridden per project.
